The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence and large language models (LLMs) has transformed modern enterprise software architecture. Over the past three years, companies have shifted from simple monolith applications to autonomous agentic systems capable of planning, tool use, and multi-step reasoning. 

However, deploying LLMs in mission-critical environments introduces unique challenges regarding latency, non-deterministic output, hallucination, and data privacy. To mitigate these risks, engineering teams are adopting hybrid approaches that combine deterministic validation rules with LLM reasoning. Furthermore, prompt chaining—where complex tasks are broken down into sequential, specialized steps—has emerged as a key technique to improve reliability and output quality over single-prompt approaches.
